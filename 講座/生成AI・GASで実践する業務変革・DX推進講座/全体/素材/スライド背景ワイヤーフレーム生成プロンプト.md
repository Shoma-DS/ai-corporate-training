# スライド背景ワイヤーフレーム生成プロンプト

- 用途: 編集可能Google Slidesテンプレートの全ページ共通背景。図解なし・文字なしのワイヤーフレームのみ。
- 保存先: `全体/素材/スライド背景ワイヤーフレーム.png`
- 生成サイズ: 1536×1024（表示時は16:9の720×405ptへ引き伸ばし）
- 生成経路: Codex App Server / GPT image 2（imagegen）。ローカル描画・SVG・HTML/CSS変換は禁止。

## 本文プロンプト

```text
Create one clean corporate slide background wireframe image for a Japanese business training deck template. Landscape 1536x1024.
This is a pure background: absolutely no text, no letters, no numbers, no words, no logos, no icons with meaning, no charts, no tables. Only subtle layout chrome.
Layout (percent of canvas height):
- Top header band from 0 to 12 percent: very light blue-gray tint (almost white), separated from the body by one thin teal horizontal rule across the full width at the 12 percent line.
- Main content area from 12 to 94 percent: pure white, completely empty.
- Bottom footer band from 94 to 100 percent: very light gray tint (almost white), with a hairline light gray top edge.
Style: isometric-corporate-clean, flat minimal corporate design, white base, navy and teal accent tones used only for the thin rule and a very small corner accent, generous whitespace.
Optional subtle decoration: an extremely subtle light-gray isometric line accent in the far bottom-right corner of the footer band, and a faint dot grid texture only inside the far left and right 2 percent margins. Keep all decoration so light that dense Japanese text and diagrams placed on top remain perfectly readable.
No gradients stronger than 5 percent, no dark areas, no shadows in the content area.
```

## ネガティブプロンプト

```text
Do not render any text, characters, numbers, Japanese or Latin letters, logos, brand marks, icons, charts, tables, placeholder boxes, dashed frames, photos, people, dark backgrounds, saturated colors, or decorative illustrations in the content area. No slide title, no page number, no watermark.
```
